SilverBP — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-05-09 · Version 1

SilverBP ("the app") is a personal blood-pressure tracking, exercise, and lifestyle-coach Android application developed by Silver ("we", "us"). Contact: shibatatsuyasilver@gmail.com. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is stored, and your choices.

1. What we collect

All data described below is collected only when you actively use the corresponding feature. The one exception is that, after you opt in to Health Connect, SilverBP periodically reads your Health Connect steps, sleep, and nutrition data in the background (via a scheduled WorkManager backfill) so your trends stay up to date. This background-read data is processed and stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us.

  • Blood-pressure readings — systolic, diastolic, pulse, posture, arm, time of day, free-text notes you enter.
  • Exercise sessions — type (walk / run), duration, distance, GPS path, calories, optional notes. GPS is recorded only while you have explicitly started a session.
  • Health Connect data (optional) — when you opt in, SilverBP reads steps, sleep, and nutrition from Health Connect, and writes blood-pressure, exercise (including route), and nutrition data back to Health Connect. With your permission it also reads these record types in the background to keep your trends current.
  • Microphone (optional) — the chat feature uses the Android Speech Recognizer to transcribe your voice into text in real time. Audio is processed by the system speech recognizer and is not stored or uploaded by SilverBP.
  • Camera (optional) — used to photograph your home blood-pressure monitor display so the on-device model can extract numbers. Photos are processed locally and saved into the app's private storage; you can delete them at any time.
  • Nickname — a short label you may enter during onboarding so the coach can address you. Optional and editable in Settings.
  • Hugging Face access token (optional) — only if you choose to download a gated Gemma model variant. Stored locally; used only for the download request.
  • LAN-sync peer keys (optional) — when you pair another device, we store the peer's public key locally so future syncs over the same Wi-Fi can be authenticated.
  • App preferences — guideline choice, coach toggles, daily targets, model picker selections.

2. How data is stored

  • All app data is kept on your device (Room database + Android DataStore), inside SilverBP's private app sandbox.
  • Health Connect data is managed by the Android Health Connect platform; SilverBP only reads or writes it with permissions you grant in the system Health Connect app.
  • SilverBP does not have a server. Your health data is never uploaded to us or to any third-party analytics or advertising service. The only place it can leave your device is the optional Google Drive backup you turn on yourself (see Network use), which uploads an encrypted snapshot to your own Drive.

3. Network use

  • Model download — when you initiate a download, the app fetches the Gemma model file over HTTPS from Hugging Face (or Google AI Edge). No personal data is sent — only your access token if you provided one.
  • Google Maps — exercise route maps are rendered by the Google Maps Android SDK. Your map view interactions are subject to Google's Privacy Policy. Recorded GPS coordinates remain on your device.
  • LAN sync (optional) — when paired with another of your devices on the same Wi-Fi, blood-pressure and exercise records are exchanged peer-to-peer over an end-to-end encrypted Noise XK channel. The data does not pass through any cloud server.
  • Cloud recognition (optional) — if you opt to use the Google Gemini cloud route for OCR, the photo of your monitor display is sent to Google's Gemini API using the API key you configured. This is governed by Google's API terms.
  • Google Drive backup (optional) — if you connect Google Drive and enable backup, SilverBP periodically uploads a .sbpbk snapshot of your health database (blood-pressure, exercise, nutrition, sleep and related records) to the hidden appDataFolder of your own Google Drive. The snapshot is end-to-end encrypted with a key derived from your recovery code before it leaves the device, so neither we nor Google can read it. To remove it: disconnect Drive and delete the backups from within SilverBP, then revoke the app's Drive access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

4. Sharing with third parties

SilverBP does not share your data with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The only third parties involved are the optional services you explicitly enable (Health Connect, Google Maps, Hugging Face downloads, Google Gemini cloud OCR), as described above.

5. Permissions

SilverBP requests the following Android permissions only when needed for a feature you use:

  • CAMERA — photograph your monitor display for OCR.
  • RECORD_AUDIO — voice input in chat (real-time transcription, not recorded).
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — GPS path tracking during an exercise session you started.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — read step count from the device's step sensor.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — show coach reminders, medication reminders, and high-reading alerts.
  • health.READ_* / health.WRITE_* — read/write the relevant Health Connect record types when you turn the feature on.
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE + ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE + ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — discover paired devices on your local Wi-Fi for LAN sync.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Every feature that uses sensitive data (Health Connect, GPS, microphone, notifications, LAN sync) can be enabled or disabled in Settings or via the system permission screen.
  • Uninstalling SilverBP removes all data stored by the app on your device. Records that were synced to Health Connect remain in Health Connect; you can remove them in the system Health Connect app.
  • You may contact us at shibatatsuyasilver@gmail.com with any privacy-related questions.

7. Children

SilverBP is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Security

  • Network traffic uses HTTPS where applicable; cleartext HTTP is disabled in the manifest.
  • LAN sync uses an end-to-end encrypted Noise XK channel with a short-authentication-string confirmation during pairing.
  • App data is stored in SilverBP's private app sandbox, accessible only to the app itself on a non-rooted device.
  • Optional app lock & encryption — you can require your fingerprint, face, or device PIN/password to open the app. When enabled, the local database and sensitive settings are additionally encrypted at rest with a key held in the device's hardware-backed keystore. This protects against device backup and file extraction on a non-rooted device; it is not designed to resist a rooted device, and the unlock credential is managed by Android (SilverBP never sees it). The feature is off by default and can be turned off again at any time.

9. Changes

We will update the version number and effective date above when this policy changes. Material changes will trigger a re-consent prompt the next time you open SilverBP.

SilverBP — 隱私政策

生效日期:2026-05-09 · 版本 1

SilverBP(以下稱「本 App」)是一款由 Silver(以下稱「我們」)所開發的個人血壓追蹤、運動與生活方式教練的 Android 應用程式。聯絡信箱:shibatatsuyasilver@gmail.com。本政策說明本 App 處理哪些資料、儲存於何處,以及您的選擇。

1. 我們蒐集哪些資料

下列資料只有在您主動使用對應功能時才會蒐集。唯一的例外是:當您啟用 Health Connect 後,本 App 會透過排程的 WorkManager 回補作業,在背景定期讀取您的 Health Connect 步數、睡眠與營養資料,讓您的趨勢保持最新。這些背景讀取的資料僅在您的裝置上處理與儲存,絕不上傳至我們。

  • 血壓讀數 — 收縮壓、舒張壓、脈搏、姿勢、左右臂、時段以及您填寫的備註。
  • 運動紀錄 — 類型(走路 / 跑步)、時間、距離、GPS 路徑、卡路里以及備註。GPS 僅在您主動開始 session 時記錄。
  • Health Connect 資料(選用) — 啟用後,本 App 會從 Health Connect 讀取步數、睡眠、營養,並把血壓、運動(含路徑)與營養資料寫回 Health Connect。經您授權後,也會在背景讀取這些資料類型,讓您的趨勢保持最新。
  • 麥克風(選用) — 聊天功能使用 Android Speech Recognizer 即時把您的語音轉成文字。音訊由系統語音辨識服務處理,本 App 不儲存、不上傳。
  • 相機(選用) — 用於拍攝家用血壓計螢幕,讓裝置端模型辨識數字。照片在本機處理並儲存於本 App 私有空間,您可隨時刪除。
  • 暱稱 — 您在引導頁可選擇輸入一個簡短稱呼,讓教練稱呼您。可選,可在設定中修改。
  • Hugging Face access token(選用) — 只有在您下載受授權保護的 Gemma 模型版本時才需要。儲存於本機,僅用於下載請求。
  • 區網同步對端公鑰(選用) — 配對另一台裝置時,我們把對端的公鑰儲存於本機,日後在同一 Wi-Fi 同步時用於認證。
  • App 偏好設定 — 高血壓指引、教練開關、每日目標、模型選擇等。

2. 資料如何儲存

  • 所有資料都儲存在您的裝置(Room 資料庫 + Android DataStore),位於 SilverBP 私有的 App sandbox 內。
  • Health Connect 資料由 Android Health Connect 平台管理;SilverBP 僅在您於系統 Health Connect App 授權後讀寫。
  • SilverBP 沒有伺服器。您的健康資料絕不上傳至我們或任何第三方分析、廣告平台。唯一可能離開您裝置的情況,是您自行開啟的選用 Google Drive 備份(請見網路使用),它會將加密後的快照上傳至您自己的 Drive。

3. 網路使用

  • 模型下載 — 當您主動下載時,本 App 透過 HTTPS 從 Hugging Face(或 Google AI Edge)取得 Gemma 模型檔。除了您所提供的 access token 之外,不會傳送任何個人資料。
  • Google 地圖 — 運動路徑地圖由 Google Maps Android SDK 繪製,適用 Google 隱私政策。您所記錄的 GPS 座標仍儲存在本機。
  • 區網同步(選用) — 在同一 Wi-Fi 上配對另一台您的裝置時,血壓與運動紀錄透過端對端加密的 Noise XK 通道直接交換。資料不經任何雲端伺服器。
  • 雲端辨識(選用) — 如您選擇使用 Google Gemini 雲端 OCR,血壓計螢幕的照片會以您所設定的 API key 傳送至 Google Gemini API,適用 Google 的 API 條款。
  • Google Drive 備份(選用) — 若您連結 Google Drive 並啟用備份,本 App 會定期將健康資料庫的 .sbpbk 快照(血壓、運動、營養、睡眠及相關紀錄)上傳至您自己 Google Drive 的隱藏 appDataFolder。快照在離開裝置前已用您的復原碼衍生的金鑰做端對端加密,因此我們與 Google 都無法讀取。若要移除:在 SilverBP 內中斷 Drive 連結並刪除備份,再到 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤銷本 App 的 Drive 存取權。

4. 第三方分享

SilverBP 不與任何第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK 分享您的資料。唯一涉及的第三方,是您主動啟用的選用服務(Health Connect、Google Maps、Hugging Face 下載、Google Gemini 雲端 OCR),如上所述。

5. 權限

本 App 僅在您使用對應功能時才請求下列 Android 權限:

  • CAMERA — 拍攝血壓計螢幕進行辨識。
  • RECORD_AUDIO — 聊天語音輸入(即時轉錄,不錄音)。
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION + FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — 您主動開始運動 session 時記錄 GPS 路徑。
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — 讀取裝置計步感測器的步數。
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — 顯示教練提醒、服藥提醒、偏高讀數警示。
  • health.READ_* / health.WRITE_* — 啟用相應功能時讀寫對應的 Health Connect 資料類型。
  • CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE + ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE + ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — 在本機 Wi-Fi 內探索已配對裝置以進行區網同步。

6. 您的選擇與權利

  • 所有使用敏感資料的功能(Health Connect、GPS、麥克風、通知、區網同步)都可在設定或系統權限頁面隨時啟用或關閉。
  • 移除 SilverBP 即清除本 App 在裝置上儲存的所有資料。已同步到 Health Connect 的紀錄會留在 Health Connect 中,您可在系統 Health Connect App 內刪除。
  • 如有任何隱私相關問題,請來信:shibatatsuyasilver@gmail.com。

7. 兒童

SilverBP 並非針對 13 歲以下兒童;我們也不會故意蒐集 13 歲以下兒童的資料。

8. 安全

  • 網路傳輸採用 HTTPS;Manifest 已停用 cleartext HTTP。
  • 區網同步採用端對端加密的 Noise XK 通道,並在配對時以 short-authentication-string 確認。
  • App 資料儲存於 SilverBP 私有 App sandbox,在未 root 的裝置上僅本 App 可存取。
  • 選用的 App 鎖定與加密 — 您可以要求開啟 App 時驗證指紋、臉部或裝置 PIN／密碼。啟用後,本機資料庫與敏感設定會額外以裝置硬體金鑰庫保管的金鑰加密儲存。此可防範未 root 裝置上的備份與檔案萃取,但不用於對抗已 root 的裝置;解鎖憑證由 Android 管理,SilverBP 永遠不會看到。此功能預設關閉,且可隨時關閉。

9. 政策變更

當本政策變更時,上方的版本號與生效日期會一併更新。重大變更會在您下次開啟 SilverBP 時觸發重新同意流程。
